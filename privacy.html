Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026 · Operator: GTA Shorts Publisher · Privacy contact: sasank98458@gmail.com

Draft for operator review. The handling described below reflects the intended local desktop workflow. Confirm it against your actual implementation before publication, especially OAuth token storage, logging, any hosted backend, analytics, and retention.

1. Overview

GTA Shorts Publisher helps creators work with locally stored video files and prepare content for authorized publishing. This policy explains the categories of data potentially handled by the Application, why they are needed, and available controls.

2. Information the Application may process

  • Video content: Finished MP4s and any related subtitles or creator-provided metadata selected for processing.
  • Generated metadata: Suggested titles, descriptions, hashtags, video tags and thumbnail frames.
  • Platform authorization: Access tokens and related authentication information received after you connect an authorized platform account.
  • Operational records: Upload identifiers, statuses, timestamps and error logs used to track uploads and prevent duplicates.

3. How data is used

The Application uses these data to prepare metadata, help transfer selected videos to connected platforms when authorized, check upload outcomes, and prevent duplicate submissions. Local AI processing is intended to run through the creator's own Ollama installation where configured.

4. Local processing and third parties

Video files and locally generated metadata may be processed on your device. When you authorize an upload, the selected video, caption and related publishing data are transferred to the relevant platform (for example, TikTok or YouTube) under that platform's own terms and privacy practices. Do not connect services or enable external AI providers without updating this policy to describe the additional transfers.

5. Storage and retention

In the described desktop setup, working files, authentication tokens and upload history can be stored on the creator's computer until removed by the creator or application maintenance. The exact storage locations and retention periods depend on deployment configuration. Review any applicable logs, caches, backups or cloud services before relying on this description.

6. Your choices and controls

You can choose which files to process, stop local automation and revoke connected-platform access from the relevant platform settings. You can also remove locally stored application files, logs or tokens from your own device, provided you understand the impact on future uploads. Revoking a token does not automatically delete previously posted videos from third-party platforms.

7. Sharing and sale of data

The core workflow is designed to transfer publishing data only to platforms you authorize and services you expressly configure. Operator confirmation required: Before publishing this policy, verify whether any hosting, telemetry, analytics, payment, third-party AI, advertising, or support services collect data, and disclose those practices here. Do not describe data as never shared or never sold unless verified.

8. Security

Platform tokens and credentials should be stored securely, excluded from public code repositories, and never embedded in publicly served web pages. No software or storage method is guaranteed completely secure. Keep your operating system and connected applications updated.

9. Children

This Application is not designed to knowingly collect information from children. Users must meet the age requirements of their connected platforms.

10. Updates and contact

This policy may be updated as functionality or data processing changes. For privacy questions or requests, contact sasank98458@gmail.com. Please include enough context to identify the application or local workflow involved, but do not send passwords or access tokens.
